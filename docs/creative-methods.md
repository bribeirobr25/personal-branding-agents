# Creative work before wording

This method defines project behavior, not a prediction of audience response. Follow [the contract](agent-contracts.md) and return all specialist work through Orchestrator.

## StoryBrief

State the audience problem, context, actual contribution, choice or experiment, alternatives, observation or result, limitations, useful lesson, evidence and disclosure status. Choose a structure that fits the facts:

- Decision -> consequence -> lesson, where a real choice exists.
- Hypothesis -> experiment -> observation -> next question, where validation is incomplete.
- Principle -> example -> application -> limits, for education.
- Before -> change -> after -> remaining uncertainty, for a demonstrable change in the same dimension.

A working artifact is not evidence of adoption, revenue or time savings. An unmeasured outcome should remain unmeasured, not become a success story.

## Distinct concepts

Hold facts, goal, audience and constraints constant. Default to three candidates only when defensible. Vary at least two of perspective, audience question, narrative structure, demonstration and format; different synonyms do not qualify. Each candidate has a promise, proof, payoff location, opening mechanism, format, risks, effort and reason it differs.

A concept may demonstrate a change, examine a trade-off or teach a reusable procedure. Do not switch the owner's topic to chase engagement. If only one defensible option exists, explain that instead of manufacturing diversity.

## Opening, understanding, retention and sharing

An opening identifies relevance for the right audience. Understanding supplies necessary context. Retention sustains interest while delivering value. Sharing has a plausible reason, such as usefulness to a colleague; it is not a guaranteed outcome.

Use four optional opening functions: identify the audience/problem; establish contextual credibility; show the route the piece will take; deliver a useful learning early. These need not be four visible paragraphs. A title, image and first sentence can complement rather than repeat each other.

For every hook ask: what is promised, where is it delivered, what supports it and what qualification must remain visible? Invented urgency, universal claims and unsupported exclusivity fail. Music, suspense and fast cuts are not universal educational quality requirements. Explain the mechanism without claiming an algorithm rewards it.

## Voice and reference use

Use current approved and rejected examples with reasons. Do not imitate another person's identity or invent emotion. Study presentation mechanisms from authorized references, including other fields, but write the expression from the current evidence and goal. Changing names in a real case does not make it synthetic.

When a view changes, preserve dates and reasons supplied by the user. Do not delete old posts or fabricate a personal transformation. Distinguish correction of fact from a stylistic preference.

## Native derivation

Source idea -> self-contained segment -> channel brief -> new artifact -> review -> exact approval. A derivative does not inherit approval from the original. Keep evidence and limitations when shortening. Choose language per client; there are no fixed language defaults.

| Format | Required check |
| --- | --- |
| Professional text | Specific useful idea, adequate context, proof and proportionate next action |
| Short post/thread | Qualifications survive compression; each part has a purpose |
| Image/carousel | Readable sequence, accessible text and meaning not conveyed by color alone |
| Short video | Context-independent opening, reading time, accurate captions and authorized audio/visuals |
| Long video/article | Depth justifies attention; evidence and structure add value rather than stretch the idea |

Platform limits and APIs must be checked at execution, not guessed from this method. Educational and entertainment goals may call for different pacing.

## Sustainable experimentation

Separate reliable approaches, small variations and exploration, but do not enforce universal content percentages before a baseline exists. Expand exploration within capacity, then focus on supported patterns. One new method increment and one content case at a time is a useful starting work-in-progress limit, not a compulsory cadence.

Apply [the concept gate](creative-concept-gate.md). After two unproductive revisions, change the premise, reduce scope, obtain evidence or ask a focused question. Do not polish indefinitely. Approve text/claims and a storyboard before costly media production, then review the actual final artifact and require new approval after material changes.
