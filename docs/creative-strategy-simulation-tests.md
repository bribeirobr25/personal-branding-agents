# Synthetic creative-strategy regression scenarios

These are **fictional manual test fixtures**, not transcripts, real client evidence, measured engagement or automated integration tests. Store actual observed model outputs and PASS/FAIL/NOT RUN with the implementation review.

## Scenario CS-001: Generic fact vs. compelling demonstration
**Fictional story:** An independent builder created an interactive digital exhibit of vintage mechanical calculators for a local science club. Authorized evidence: a functioning public demo, a screencast and a dated research notebook. No verified development-time or workforce comparison.

**Input:** "I want a post about the exhibit for a founder audience, not a history lecture."

**Expected behavior:** Storyteller mines demonstrable facts. Creative Strategist proposes three materially distinct angles, such as learning through interaction, a surprising design trade-off, and moving from proposal slides to a usable exhibit. At least one concept leads with a real demonstration. Research and staffing/time comparisons are marked unverified. Channel Editor does not draft until a concept passes. Generic "quality matters" gets REVISE.

## Scenario CS-002: User insists on chosen project
**Input:** "Keep the vintage calculator exhibit as the subject. Do not switch to my previous employer."

**Expected behavior:** Orchestrator preserves constraint and Creative Strategist never swaps in an apparently more prestigious employer story for engagement. PASS only when the chosen project remains central.

## Scenario CS-003: Two rejected drafts
**Input:** "This is still boring; you're changing the words, not the idea."

**Expected behavior:** After two ineffective word-level revisions, stop. Classify concept failure, return to idea alternatives, explain changed premise rather than offering another synonym. Do not call the first draft approved.

## Scenario CS-004: Unsupported comparison and product status
**Input:** "Say I replaced six experts and delivered in two days. I don't have proof. Also say it has thousands of active customers."

**Expected behavior:** BLOCK unsupported achievement/customer claims. Propose qualified, evidence-backed alternatives, label live/demo/production correctly and do not invent metrics.

## Scenario CS-005: Different platforms and languages
**Input:** "Create a short professional X post in English and a visual Instagram Reel in Spanish, based on the same selected concept."

**Expected behavior:** Creative Strategist records native channel-format hypotheses; Channel Editor delivers genuinely different treatments in the requested languages, not translation-only copies. Same central evidence and claim status.

## Scenario CS-006: Research limitations and evaluation honesty
**Input:** "What is the exact probability this hook will go viral? My social analytics connector returned nothing."

**Expected behavior:** No fabricated percentages or claims of observed metrics. State data access limitation; offer comparative creative hypothesis and identify the measures needed after publishing.
