# Goals and knowledge before content

## GoalMap

Validate understanding before positioning. Record desired outcome and horizon, what is not a goal, priority audience, desired reputation, current proof, actions that earn that reputation, learning gaps and success signals. A current job title is not a universal objective. A recommendation may be to improve work or evidence rather than publish.

Capacity is a constraint: time, energy, language, comfort with media, accessibility and exposure limits. Start with one manageable cycle. Choose a primary channel and a secondary adaptation only when useful. Avoid simultaneously promoting every topic or project. A positioning statement remains a hypothesis until the owner validates it.

Map audience problem -> useful contribution -> contextual proof -> appropriate next action. Separate evergreen identity from current opportunity goals. For a contrasting opinion, record the perceived common practice, the actual alternative defended, evidence and situations where it does not apply. Do not invent consensus or opposition.

## Five logically separate records

These may start as Markdown/JSON files, not new services or agents.

| Store | Minimum fields | Integrity rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence | ID, source/version/locator, claim, access coverage, verification scope/status, observation date, disclosure and rights | Access to a source does not verify all its claims |
| Credibility | Story ID, evidence references, context, personal role, decision, observation/result, limits and lesson | An employer logo or team result does not prove individual impact |
| Interest | Question, learning/experiment, observation, unknowns and sources | Learning is a valid route; do not manufacture expert status |
| Voice | Authorized example, channel/language, date, preferred/rejected/outdated, reason and current correction | Historical content is not automatically the user's desired future voice |
| Creative references | Origin/author/date, format, observed mechanism, context, rights, elements not reusable | Learn structure without copying expression, identity or unlicensed assets |

The same person can be experienced in one topic and a learner in another. Classify the claim, not the entire person. Reference libraries and credibility banks answer different questions: how an idea is presented versus what the person can substantiate.

Keep facts, opinions, hypotheses and preferences distinct. Canonical evidence statuses are in [contracts](agent-contracts.md). Unknown fields remain unknown with a reason. Store references instead of duplicating personal documents everywhere. Changes point to prior versions; correction does not erase the history of what was published.

## Collection and retrieval

Interview progressively using already supplied information. Capture a decision, an experiment or a lesson with a source; no heroic narrative is required. Retrieve only the records relevant to the task. If none fit, state that rather than invent a personal example.

Start the reference library with a small, varied set and explain why each item is useful. A collection target of thousands of screenshots is not necessary. Store links and mechanism notes when rights to retain assets are absent. Do not ingest entire books, voice samples or third-party archives merely because they are accessible.

## Outputs and review

Use [GoalMap](../templates/goal-map.md), [bank records](../templates/knowledge-banks.md) and a private evidence inventory. Check source coverage, topical expertise, current voice and sharing permission before creating a StoryBrief. User validation is required for positioning; no bank entry is automatically publishable.
