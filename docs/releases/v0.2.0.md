# Migrating to method 0.2.0

## Compatibility and migration

- Stable task request/response envelopes remain. New domain records and canonical vocabulary require explicit adaptation.
- Normalize legacy `user-reported` to `user_reported` and content `draft` to `drafted`, recording migration.
- Legacy pre-draft PASS maps to SELECT eligibility, never owner approval; final gates keep PASS / REVISE / BLOCK.
- Preserve scheduled between approved and published, without inferring publication from elapsed time.
- Add concept_selected for new cycles, not retroactive gate results for old content.
- ApprovalRecord now requires `actor_role` and private `authority_ref` in addition to exact artifact/hash/action/destination. Unknown authority cannot authorize a new action; historical records are not rewritten as if new fields were originally present.
- Keep task/access/evidence/concept/content/action states distinct. Unknown restrictions/schemas fail safely.
- Preserve historical CSV rows and evidence. Old architecture/onboarding heading versions were document iterations, not release tags.

## Adopt or roll back

Pin the published release tag and its full commit SHA in your private workspace. Compare the framework contracts, templates and workflows with your local adaptations before switching. Keep your private evidence and approvals with the workspace. To roll back, restore the previous pin and its compatible adaptations.
