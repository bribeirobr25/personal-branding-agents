# Method 0.6.0 unreleased draft

This note is a draft. It is not a published release, not a tag, and not approval to merge.

Method 0.6.0 adds the Positioning phase in [the positioning workflow](../../workflows/positioning.md). Discovery stays the method 0.5.3 procedure in [onboarding](../../workflows/onboarding.md), with a hand-off after a direction is chosen. Discovery wording is otherwise unchanged. De-duplication of repeated Discovery rules is not in this draft.

Contract version moves from 0.2.0 to 0.3.0. The new vocabulary is Positioning `record_state`: `working`, `validated`, `superseded`. Task envelopes, evidence statuses, content states, final gates, and approval fields keep their 0.2.0 meaning. `validated` on a Positioning record is not an `ApprovalRecord`.

**Status:** Not validated. Checked only with forced-case simulations on one model family (same-model and structural only). The full forced run did not meet its safety bar; a targeted re-test of the fix met it. Not independently validated.

The blank positioning template is not rewritten. The working-copy shape lives in the Positioning workflow. The expression plan (themes, channels, voice, offers, funnels, and public profile copy) is named and not built. The content cycle starts once a channel exists. Until the expression plan is built, the person names the channel.

## Adopt or roll back

Do not adopt this draft as a published pin. The last published method is 0.5.3. After a later publication, pin that tag and its full commit SHA in your private workspace. Roll back by restoring the prior pin and compatible overrides.

Records written under contract 0.2.0 stay valid. New Positioning working copies use contract 0.3.0 and `record_state`. Do not rewrite old records as if `record_state` had been present. For the 0.5.3 Discovery behavior, see [method 0.5.3](v0.5.3.md). For older records, see [migration to 0.2.0](v0.2.0.md).
