## Evidence boundaries

All persona descriptions and documents are invented. No URL was fetched and no real account, credential, private person's data or source materials were used. Statements such as 'I have not accessed' are true of the scenario simulation; no live connector authorization was exercised. Both arms were drafted in one author context knowing the rules. This file is suitable for qualitative comparison of *examples*, not for statistical outcome estimates or safety certification.
