For problem-solving contracts, make it easy for a buyer to recognize **a problem you can tackle**, rather than presenting yourself as a general problem-solver.

Use this provisional offer structure:

> “When **[specific situation]** causes **[costly problem]**, I help **[type of client]** diagnose what’s going wrong and develop **[concrete deliverable]**. Start with a fixed-scope diagnostic.”

In your five minutes, write one short example:
- **Problem:** What was stuck?
- **Your contribution:** What did you personally investigate, decide or change?
- **Evidence:** What result can you honestly support—or what did you learn?

Then turn it into a low-risk starting offer: **a diagnostic with a clear deliverable, deadline and fee**, rather than an open-ended promise to solve everything.
