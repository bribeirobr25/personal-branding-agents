**Offer a paid diagnosis before a large project.** It gives a prospective client a smaller commitment and gives you a defined scope.

Use this template:

> “Before committing to a larger project, I offer a fixed-scope diagnostic of [problem]. You receive an assessment of likely causes, prioritized recommendations, and a proposed scope for any follow-on work. The diagnostic stands alone—there’s no obligation to continue.”

Set the fee and timeline only after you understand the scope. Promise the assessment, not a guaranteed fix.
