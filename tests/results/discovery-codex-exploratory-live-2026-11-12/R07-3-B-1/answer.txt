With five minutes, focus on **one concrete problem a client could hire you to solve**, rather than a broad “problem-solver” brand.

Use this as a provisional offer—not a claim about experience you haven’t shared:

> “I help [type of client] tackle [specific recurring problem]. We start with a fixed-scope diagnostic, then agree on an implementation contract if there’s a clear fit.”

A practical first contract could include:
- **Scope:** investigate one defined problem.
- **Deliverables:** diagnosis, prioritized recommendations, and an implementation plan.
- **Boundary:** no guaranteed business outcome; implementation is priced separately.

For outreach to an existing contact:

> “I’m looking for contract work tackling [specific problem]. If that’s a current issue for your team, I can propose a small, paid diagnostic with clear deliverables before either side commits to a larger project.”

Choose a problem you’ve actually handled, and support it with one truthful example of your role and what happened. This is a starting template, not validated positioning; I don’t yet know your experience.
