# Workflow: Content cycle

```text
evidence → idea → brief → draft → five-gate review
                                      |
                           revise if needed
                                      |
                           human approval
                                      |
                          manual publication
                                      |
                          record + analyze
```

1. **Select evidence:** choose a publishable insight linked to verified or clearly qualified experience.
2. **Brief:** fill `templates/content-brief.md` in private workspace.
3. **Draft:** Channel Editor writes platform- and language-specific text.
4. **Review:** Quality Reviewer completes `templates/review-report.md`. Any BLOCK prevents publication.
5. **Approve:** owner explicitly approves the **exact final revision**.
6. **Publish:** manual in v0.1, or an external integration only with separately authorized action.
7. **Track:** log URL, date, time zone and observed outcomes in the private tracker.
8. **Learn:** Analyst proposes next test based on evidence, not vanity metrics alone.

Never treat a draft as scheduled or published without confirmation from the relevant system.
