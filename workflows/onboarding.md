# Workflow: Guided onboarding

**Goal:** produce an evidence-aware positioning strategy, without requiring a long intake form.

## Step 1: Set goals
Ask what professional outcomes matter most (e.g. career opportunities, consulting, thought leadership), who should recognize the client and in what markets. Allow multiple goals.

## Step 2: Choose channels
Confirm channels, languages and desired effort. Do not assume the same tone on every platform.

## Step 3: Gather only necessary context
Request a short professional background, 2–3 examples of meaningful work, current projects, public links (if authorized), areas of expertise and topics to avoid. Incomplete is okay; label gaps.

## Step 4: Evidence audit
Run [Researcher](../agents/researcher.md). Do not publish unverified achievements.

## Step 5: Define positioning
Run [Positioning Strategist](../agents/positioning-strategist.md). Present trade-offs and request human approval.

## Step 6: Establish private workspace
Copy `templates/` **outside** the public repository. Store client details, consent and decision log there. Use least-necessary input with AI services.

## Step 7: Launch editorial experiment
Choose 3 story ideas with evidence. Enter [content cycle](content-cycle.md). No automatic posting.

## Completion criteria
A private profile, evidence inventory and owner-reviewed positioning exist; missing proof and approval steps are explicit.
